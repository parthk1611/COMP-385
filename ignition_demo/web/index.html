Understand the system

A model you can trace

36 inputs describe seasonality, terrain, prior-day weather and earlier fire history. Histogram gradient boosting combines them into a daily ignition score. The model was fitted on 2010–2016 data.

What does a score mean?

A score of 0.5% represents the model’s estimated probability of at least one recorded ignition in that cell during that day. A high rank can still have a small absolute probability. ROC-AUC is a ranking statistic, not alert accuracy.

Why use F2 for the demo?

F2 puts more weight on recall than precision. We selected the best F2 among eight existing cutoffs using 2018 only. It illustrates a recall-focused decision, while the visible counts show its substantial false-alert and missed-fire burden.

Read the limits with the result

    Reconstructed results are labelled separately from the original published experiment. This app demonstrates a historical model and does not provide emergency guidance.
