Fire Spread: AI vs Physics

Ontario wildfires from 2021 to 2024 that neither model saw while it was built. At each step, both the AI model (a CNN) and the FBP physics simulator start from where satellites last saw the fire and predict the same number of 375 m cells. The next satellite pass then shows where the fire actually spread.
