usage: run_spread_replay.py [-h] [--calibration-fires CALIBRATION_FIRES]
                            [--holdout-fires HOLDOUT_FIRES] [--seed SEED]
                            [--workers WORKERS] [--output-dir OUTPUT_DIR]

Replay real fires through the simulator and score it against the satellite
record. For each fire, and each pair of consecutive satellite observations,
the simulator starts from the area burned up to the first observation, runs
for the hours until the second, and is scored on the new cells it predicts
against the new cells the satellite saw. Scoring is on the satellite's own 375
m grid. A satellite pass shows only where fire is active at that moment, so
the cells seen so far are a patchy under-count of what has burned. The
starting area is therefore those cells with the gaps between them closed and
holes filled. For the same reason the observed new cells are a lower bound:
the fair questions are whether the simulator reaches them (recall), how far
its head runs against theirs, and whether it runs the same way. Two things are
done here, in order: 1. two settings are tuned on a sample of calibration
fires: a scale on every rate of spread, and a further scale on the flank and
back rates, to the values at which the simulated head runs as far as the
observed one and the simulated area matches what the satellite record implies;
2. the tuned simulator is scored once on a sample of holdout fires. Each
result is set beside a baseline that grows the fire by the same area evenly in
all directions, so the score shows what wind, fuel and terrain add. Needs the
packages listed in requirements.txt, including rasterio.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --calibration-fires CALIBRATION_FIRES
  --holdout-fires HOLDOUT_FIRES
  --seed SEED
  --workers WORKERS
  --output-dir OUTPUT_DIR
