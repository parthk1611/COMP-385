usage: export_spread_viewer_data.py [-h] [--fires-per-split FIRES_PER_SPLIT]
                                    [--page-only] [--no-base-map]
                                    [--simulation-dir SIMULATION_DIR]

Export simulator runs for the browser viewer. Replays a few fires with the
tuned settings and writes `viewer/fire_spread_viewer.html`: the page in
`viewer/viewer_template.html` with the data written into it, so the one file
opens anywhere. For each fire it keeps the fuel grid, where satellites saw the
fire at each pass, the simulated arrival time of every cell for each step
between passes, the hourly wind, and the step's scores. It also runs the first
six hours from the reported ignition point. The ground under the fire is the
Canada Base Map (see `spread_basemap.py`): one image for each fire's window
and a closer one around its ignition run, downloaded the first time they are
needed and kept in `data/raw/basemap/`. Fires are chosen by rule, not by how
well they turned out: in each split, the scored fires with the most usable
steps whose grid is small enough to ship.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --fires-per-split FIRES_PER_SPLIT
  --page-only           rebuild the page from the template and the data
                        already exported, without simulating again
  --no-base-map         leave out the Canada Base Map images; the viewer then
                        draws the fuel map as its ground
  --simulation-dir SIMULATION_DIR
