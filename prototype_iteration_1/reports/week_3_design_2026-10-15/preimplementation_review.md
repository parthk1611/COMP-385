# Week 3 design review before implementation

The ignition comparison uses the unchanged historical v1 hindcast. Its 2010–2019 row universe, source vintages and known station/mask limitations remain explicit. The selected seven-source enrichment is a separate unfinished build. Neither the 2025 weather pilot nor the 2024 fuel map will enter this ignition experiment.

The local machine has 18 GiB RAM. Fit all four candidates on the same deterministic case-control training sample: every positive and approximately 1% of negatives from 2010–2015. Weight retained negatives by eligible negative population divided by retained negatives; retain unit positive weights. This restores the training population's event proportion in the weighted loss, but does not make sampled training equivalent to full-data fitting. Evaluate every eligible 2016–2017 validation row, without negative sampling. Record the full counts and sample hashes.

Compare constant historical rate, median-imputed/scaled logistic regression, native-missing-value histogram gradient boosting and LightGBM. Fixed settings are recorded before fitting. Fit transformations on training only. Do not use class balancing as well as sampling correction. Primary metric is average precision; report prevalence, ROC-AUC, Brier score, log loss, year-level stability and recall/precision at a fixed 1% daily alert budget. These are development results, not final-test performance or calibrated operational probabilities.

Two comparisons are required. The temporal comparison uses all grid cells with 36 approved predictors. The second creates a deterministic inner 100 km blocked split within the frozen outer spatial-development cells, adds a 20 km centre buffer, fits only inner-training cells in 2010–2015 and validates only inner-validation cells in 2016–2017. It excludes all five fire-history predictors. The inner-spatial experiment excludes the frozen outer geographic test/buffer cells. The separate temporal experiment retains its original all-geography protocol. Neither experiment opens 2018 calibration or 2019 final-test content. Inner geographic validation supports design selection without spending the reserved outer test.

Implementation will read only training and validation Parquet files after validating their manifest/selector hashes. It must reject role/year mismatch, forbidden predictor names and feature-order mismatch. Save candidates, environment/config/source hashes, full validation scores and save/load parity evidence to a separate Week 3 experiment directory. No scoring server, map, SHAP service or live adapter is implied.

Spread review will separate canonical FBP equations from the implemented subset, grid propagation, seasonal assumptions, reconstructed fuels, weather timing and observation-based initialization. A surface simulator without spotting must not cross a full-domain one-cell water barrier. Reproduce and fix that software defect with regression tests. Preserve saved v1 replay scores as evidence for the old code; do not relabel them as scores for the fixed implementation. Final-perimeter-assisted fuel repair remains reconstruction-only, never accepted as forecast input. A documented rejection of forecast readiness is a completed design-review outcome, not an operational acceptance.

Accepted for these bounded local experiments. Professor/team approval, full architecture acceptance for the live application and final deployment remain outside this review.
